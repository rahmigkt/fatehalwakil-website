PORT SUDAN & ATBARA FIELD OPERATION

April 22 – May 4, 2026

Completed

ABOUT THE OPERATION

A field relief operation was carried out in Port Sudan and Atbara for families displaced by the ongoing conflict in Sudan. The operation delivered hot meals, food parcels, emergency medical supplies, clothing and cash assistance directly to families and refugee camps.

2,100Food Parcels Distributed
2Field Regions
13Days of Operation

TIMELINE OF ACTIVITIES

  • April 22, 2026 — Port Sudan & Atbara The field team arrived in Port Sudan; after obtaining official permits, they traveled on to Atbara.
  • April 24, 2026 — Organization Building & Refugee Camp The first distribution of food parcels for 600 people took place at the organization's center and the refugee camp.
  • April 25, 2026 — Atbara State Teaching & Research Hospital The hospital was visited to assess needs on-site; support was provided to patients and their companions.
  • April 29 – May 1, 2026 — Refugee Camp & Organization Building Of the 600 food parcels prepared, 300 were delivered at the organization's building and the remaining 300 to camp residents.
  • May 2–4, 2026 — Home Visits 300 food parcels, clothing and cash support were delivered directly to homes through family visits.
  • May 3, 2026 — Refugee Camp A sacrificial animal was slaughtered and the meat distributed; on the same day, camp residents received clothing, food parcels and cash assistance.

A letter of appreciation was received from Atbara State Teaching & Research Hospital, visited during the operation. Field distributions were video-documented for transparency.
