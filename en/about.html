ABOUT US

The story of years of dedicated work in Sudan becoming a formally registered organization.

OUR STORY

The roots of Fateh Al-Wakil Organization for Sustainable Development go back long before its official registration. Over the years, hundreds of individual and voluntary humanitarian activities were carried out for vulnerable communities in Sudan.

This experience was brought under a formal structure in 2024, when the organization was registered as a national voluntary organization with the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) under Sudan's Council of Ministers, registration No. 436/2024. The registration was made under the 2006 Voluntary and Humanitarian Work Act and its regulations.

Today, our organization continues to reach displaced families and vulnerable communities in Sudan in the fields of health, emergency humanitarian aid, and sustainable community development.

Official Registration: Sudan's Council of Ministers Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC), NGOs Registrar General — No. 436/2024
