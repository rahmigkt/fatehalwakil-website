Years on the Ground in Sudan

Before and after official registration

Fateh Al-Wakil's roots go back to voluntary work in Sudan that began long before the organization's official founding.

Over the years, hundreds of activities were carried out directly with communities in need on the ground.

Today, this experience continues under a formal institutional structure.

We Are an Officially Registered Organization

With Sudan's Council of Ministers

Our organization is registered as a national voluntary organization by the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) under Sudan's Council of Ministers, registration No. 436/2024.

The registration was made in accordance with the 2006 Voluntary and Humanitarian Work Act and its regulations.

This official status allows us to carry out our work on a more transparent and institutional basis.

About Us

Fateh Al-Wakil Organization for Sustainable Development is a nationally registered voluntary organization working in health, emergency relief, and community development in Sudan, registered with the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) under No. 436/2024.

Our Secretary General, Dr. Umeyma Mohammed Al-Hajj Abdullah, graduated from Istanbul University Faculty of Medicine, Department of Dentistry, in 1991, and while working as a dentist in Turkey has led hundreds of humanitarian activities in Sudan over the years. The organization's field operations are managed by Director Sitna Mohammed Al-Hajj Abdullah.
